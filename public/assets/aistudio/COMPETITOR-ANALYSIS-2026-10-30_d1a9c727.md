# Apano Aviation: competitor analysis and LinkedIn competitor list (30 Oct 2026)

**Built from:** the 5 Oct Competitor Map (162 entries: DataForSEO Google India data, Meta Ad Library, web search), the 5 Oct webinar competitor sweep, fresh web searches on 30 Oct, and each shortlisted competitor's own website (homepage text and footer links).
**Total competitors listed:** 176 (162 from the map + 14 found today). Full roster in the appendix.
**What I could not do:** open LinkedIn itself. The built-in browser is not signed in to LinkedIn, and I don't enter passwords. So follower counts are missing, and the LinkedIn URLs below come from links on each competitor's own website. Where a site has no LinkedIn link, the entry says "search by name".

## 1. LinkedIn: what you can actually add

LinkedIn changed competitor analytics on 15 Oct 2025:

- **Free Company Page:** tracks **1** competitor.
- **Premium Company Page:** tracks up to **9**.

Where: your Page admin view → **Analytics → Competitors → Edit competitors**, then search by company name and check the logo and URL match.

Benchmarking is most useful against pages close to your size and job. Apano's page has 56 followers (Sept), so giant portals like Shiksha or Careers360 would only show you a gap you can't act on. The list below favours companies that do the same job as Apano or chase the same students.

## 3. How the market is shaped

Apano competes in five rings. Only the first does exactly what Apano does.

| Ring | Who | What they want from the same student | Threat to Apano |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Neutral guides / directories** | The Pilot's Compass, Vayupath, Aviation Path, Aviation Ivy, The Flying Engineer, BestAviation.net, FlyingSchools.in, PilotCareer.in | "Let us help you choose a school" | **High.** Same promise, same trust language. Pilot's Compass also sells ground classes, so it isn't fully neutral either. |
| **B. Big education portals** | Shiksha, Collegedunia, Careers360, Justdial, CollegeDekho, Getmyuni, Sulekha | Own the "aviation courses after 12th" type searches and sell leads to institutes | **Medium.** Huge SEO reach, but generic and not aviation specialists. Apano's depth (DGCA re-attempt help, honest fees) beats them on trust. |
| **C. Ground schools with content engines** | FMS, ThePilot.in, Golden Epaulettes, Top Crew, Wing Path, PilotCET, Poetic Pilot, CNTAA, Aviator Cloud, Wind Chasers | Win the student early with free guides, quizzes, webinars, then sell their own course | **High** on search and webinars. They outrank Apano on almost every pilot query and give webinars away free. |
| **D. Flying schools and abroad training** | VFTI, Dunes, Orient, Redbird, Bombay Flying Club, Veritastech, BAA Training, Flying Academy, International Aero Academy, Florida Flyers | Enrol the student directly | **Low to medium.** Many are potential Apano listings or panel partners, not just rivals. |
| **E. Cabin crew / ground staff academies** | Frankfinn, Skytech, Aptech Aviation, RiseUp, Aikon, APT Advantage, Fly Higher, Dfly Ahmedabad | Class 12 students and their parents, often in Gujarat | **Medium.** Heavy Meta spend; ads lead on price ("₹45,000*") and parent fears. |

## 4. Closest competitors, up close

**The Pilot's Compass.** Homepage today: "Compare Schools · Book Counselling · Ground Classes (NEW) · Become a Pilot". A global directory of FTOs with DGCA rating, fleet, approval number and phone, plus featured US (World Wide Wings, Kingsky) and European (BAA Training) schools. Meta ads in October pushed a Young Aviators Camp and "DGCA Batch 2 Enrolments Open". Strength: structured school data (DGCA rating, fleet), naval-aviator founder story. Weakness: it now sells its own ground classes, which undercuts the "unbiased" claim. Apano's angle: stay the guide that doesn't sell a course of its own, and say so.

**Vayupath.** Positions as a public-interest career map (run by the Centre for Scientific Outreach): 16 sectors, salary ranges ₹20k–12L/month, coverage of 36 states and UTs, free counsellor, institute listings, school seminars, job board. Strength: breadth and a non-commercial feel. Weakness: shallow on pilot specifics, fees and DGCA exams. Watch it: it is chasing the same institute-listing revenue Apano wants.

**FMS Aviation Academy.** Search leader for "aviation courses after 12th" (#1) and online CPL (#8), cited in Google's AI Overview, and runs "Find Your Perfect Flying School" ads. It acts like a discovery platform at the top of the funnel, then sells its own ground school.

**ThePilot.in / Golden Epaulettes.** Content-first ground schools. They publish the fee breakdowns and "how to choose a flight school" guides Apano should own. Their fee numbers disagree with each other (Golden Epaulettes ~₹55L all-in, others ₹45–65L), which is the confusion Apano can clear up.

**Right Source Aviation, AeroMitra, Wind Chasers, Veritastech.** All run **free** pilot career webinars aimed at students and parents. AeroMitra and Wind Chasers also offer free counselling and eligibility quizzes. This is why ₹99 webinars are hard to sell.

**Frankfinn / Skytech / Gujarat cabin crew academies.** Frankfinn franchise pages (incl. Rajkot) run Hinglish ads like "12th Ke Baad High-Paying Career". Skytech, Aptech Aviation, Infinifly, RiseUp and Dfly all have Ahmedabad centres. These are the rivals in Apano's own city.

## 5. Where Apano stands (numbers from the 5 Oct map)

- **Search:** ~3.4k organic visits/month and 361 ranked keywords, against VFTI ~97k, Dunes ~64k, FMS ~61k, Skytech ~46k, ThePilot.in ~36k, Top Crew ~30k. Position 38 for "best flying schools in india".
- **One win:** Apano's /cpl-online page is cited in Google's AI Overview for "cpl ground classes online".
- **Paid:** Frankfinn, Speedjet, Aviacons, Amty, FMS and Top Crew run the most Meta ads in the samples.
- **LinkedIn:** 56 followers (Sept). Most competitors above link a LinkedIn page from their site; many smaller ad-led academies don't have one at all, so LinkedIn is a channel where Apano can stand out cheaply.

## 6. Gaps Apano can own

1. **Being truly neutral.** Almost every "guidance" rival also sells training (Pilot's Compass ground classes, FMS, Top Crew, RSA). Apano can say plainly that it doesn't run a flying school or ground school.
2. **DGCA re-attempt help.** No rival runs an event or programme on failed papers (from the 5 Oct sweep).
3. **One honest fee comparison.** Rivals publish clashing totals (₹40L to ₹1Cr+). A sourced, side-by-side fee table is the most useful thing Apano could publish.
4. **Gujarat, in Gujarati.** National players run Hindi/English. Apano's Gujarati CTWA reels already cost ₹23–26 per chat.
5. **LinkedIn itself.** Rivals' LinkedIn activity looks thin from their sites. Track the nine above and see who posts.

## 7. Caveats

- Traffic and keyword numbers are DataForSEO estimates from 5 Oct, not measured.
- Claims quoted from rivals' sites (student counts, pass rates, partnerships) are not verified.
- Meta ad counts are samples, so treat them as a floor.
- LinkedIn URLs come from each company's own website; I couldn't open LinkedIn to confirm them or read follower counts.
- "Direct / overlap / adjacent" is a judgement call.
